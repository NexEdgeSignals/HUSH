# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 04-10-2026**

Your privacy is the whole reason HUSH exists, so here's an honest, plain-language explanation of what we do — and don't do — with your information.

**What HUSH never does:**
- We never collect your messages, your contacts, or any other personal information.
- We don't run any server that your messages pass through — there's simply nothing for us to see.
- We don't ask you to create an account, verify a phone number, or connect to the internet just to send a text.
- We never share, sell, or hand off your data to anyone else, for the simple reason that we never have it in the first place.

**What HUSH actually does:**
- HUSH encrypts your messages on your own device before they're sent, using the same trusted encryption approach the original Silence project relied on.
- HUSH sends and receives your messages through your phone's normal SMS/MMS service, the same way any other texting app does.
- Everything HUSH stores — your message history, your contacts, your encryption keys — stays only on your own device, in your own hands.

**Permissions HUSH asks for, and why:**
To work as your everyday messaging app, HUSH asks for a few permissions on your phone:
- **SMS** — to send and receive your text messages.
- **Contacts** — so you can pick who to message by name instead of typing numbers.
- **Phone** — so HUSH can work properly alongside your phone's calling features and identify your own number.
- **Media Files** — so you can send and receive photos, videos, and other attachments.

HUSH only ever uses these permissions to make the app work for you. None of them are used to collect or send your information anywhere else.

**Open, for anyone to check:**
HUSH is free, open-source software under the GPLv3. Its entire source code is out in the open for anyone to read, question, or improve.

**Questions?**
If anything here isn't clear, please open an issue on the project's GitHub page — we're happy to explain further.
