# About HUSH

**HUSH**
Version 1.0.0

Welcome to HUSH — a free, open-source, end-to-end encrypted SMS and MMS app for Android, made to help you text the people you care about without anyone else listening in.

HUSH carries forward the spirit of Silence (formerly SMSSecure), a much-loved privacy-focused messaging app whose development came to a stop several years ago. We didn't want that work to simply disappear, so HUSH picks it back up and keeps it alive and working on today's phones.

Every message you send through HUSH is encrypted right on your own device, and only the person you're writing to can ever read it. HUSH works over your phone's regular SMS/MMS network, so there's no internet connection to worry about, no account to create, and no company's server sitting in the middle of your conversation.

HUSH is, and always will be, free and open source — licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3). We're grateful to the original Silence, SMSSecure, and TextSecure projects and everyone who built and maintained them before us; HUSH simply wouldn't exist without their work.
